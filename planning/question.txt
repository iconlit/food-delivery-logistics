==============================================================================
FOOD DELIVERY LOGISTICS - BUSINESS QUESTIONS BY STAKEHOLDER
==============================================================================
Dataset: Food_Delivery_Time_Prediction.csv
Scope:   50,000 orders, Jan - Dec 2025
Note:    Every question below can be answered using the columns in the dataset.
         Figures in brackets are quick reference values from the data.

Dataset columns used:
Order_ID, Order_Date, Order_Hour, Day_of_Week, Is_Weekend, Is_Festival,
Weather, Pickup_Zone, Dropoff_Zone, Vehicle_Type, Rider_Experience_Years,
Rider_Rating, Restaurant_Rating, Cuisine_Type, Order_Items, Restaurant_Load,
Preparation_Time_Min, Road_Distance_km, Delivery_Distance_Category,
Traffic_Level, Number_of_Signals, Average_Speed_kmph, Delivery_Priority,
Time_taken_min

Overall reference: average delivery time = 84 min; 35% of orders take over
90 min; 14% take over 120 min.


==============================================================================
1. SHAREHOLDERS / INVESTORS
==============================================================================
Main concern: Is the delivery operation reliable, scalable and well run?

Q1.  How many orders do we handle each month, and is volume growing or flat?
     [Columns: Order_Date, Order_ID]
Q2.  What share of orders are delivered late (over 90 and over 120 minutes)?
     [Columns: Time_taken_min]
Q3.  Is our average delivery time improving or getting worse over the year?
     [Columns: Order_Date, Time_taken_min]
Q4.  How much of our delay comes from things we cannot control (weather,
     traffic) versus things we can control (vehicle choice, rider, zone)?
     [Columns: Weather, Traffic_Level, Vehicle_Type, Rider_Experience_Years]
Q5.  Which days and conditions create the biggest operational risk
     (festivals, storms, severe traffic)?
     [Columns: Is_Festival, Weather, Traffic_Level, Time_taken_min]
Q6.  How consistent is our service? How wide is the gap between our fastest
     and slowest deliveries?
     [Columns: Time_taken_min]

==============================================================================
Main concern: Are we meeting our delivery promise, and where should we focus?

Q1.  What is our average delivery time, and what delivery time can we

     [Columns: Road_Distance_km, Average_Speed_kmph, Preparation_Time_Min,

     Weekends run about 8 min slower; festivals about 12 min slower.

Q4.  Which zones and routes perform best and worst?
     [Columns: Pickup_Zone, Dropoff_Zone, Time_taken_min]

     [Columns: Delivery_Priority, Time_taken_min]
     [VIP 82 min vs Normal 84 min - barely different]
Q6.  Can we predict delivery time accurately at the moment an order is
     placed, so customers get a reliable ETA?
     [Columns: all input columns vs Time_taken_min]

==============================================================================
SUGGESTED FIRST ANALYSES (highest business impact)
==============================================================================
1. Vehicle vs distance: bicycles take 130 min on routes bikes finish in 72.
2. Priority / VIP tiers show almost no speed advantage over Normal orders.
3. Weather and traffic: storms add about 37 min and severe traffic about
   58 min versus the best conditions.
4. Peak-hour slowdowns at 08:00-10:00 and 18:00-21:00.
5. CBD pickups are roughly 11-16 min slower than other zones.
6. Slow cuisines (Biryani, North Indian) driven by preparation time.
